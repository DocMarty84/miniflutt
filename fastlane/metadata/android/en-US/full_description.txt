<p><i>Miniflutt</i> is an Android client for the self-hosted Miniflux feed reader. Compatible with Miniflux 2.0.21+.</p><p><br><b>Features:</b></p><ul><li>online reading</li><li>audio and video playback</li><li>article search</li><li>dark theme available on night mode</li><li>sort, search, grouping and more</li><li>100% free and open-source</li><li>no ads</li></ul><p>Limitation: being online is required (no fetching for offline reading).</p>
